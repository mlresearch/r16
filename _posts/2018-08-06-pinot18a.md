abstract: In this paper, we present the first differentially private clustering
  method for arbitraryshaped node clusters in a graph. This algorithm takes as
  input only an approximate Minimum Spanning Tree (MST) T released under weight
  differential privacy constraints from the graph. Then, the underlying nonconvex
  clustering partition is successfully recovered from cutting optimal cuts on T
  . As opposed to existing methods, our algorithm is theoretically well-motivated.
  Experiments support our theoretical findings.
