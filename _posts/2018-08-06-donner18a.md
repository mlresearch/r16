abstract: We reconsider a nonparametric density model based on Gaussian processes.
  By augmenting the model with latent P{ó}lya–Gamma random variables and a latent
  marked Poisson process we obtain a new likelihood which is conjugate to the model’s
  Gaussian process prior. The augmented posterior allows for efficient infer- ence
  by Gibbs sampling and an approximate variational mean field approach. For the latter
  we utilise sparse GP approximations to tackle the infinite dimensionality of the
  problem. The performance of both algorithms and compar- isons with other density
  estimators are demon- strated on artificial and real datasets with up to several
  thousand data points.
