abstract: We consider the classical stochastic multi-armed bandit but where, from
  time to time and roughly with frequency $\epsilon$, an extra observation is gathered
  by the agent for free. We prove that, no matter how small $\epsilon$ is the agent
  can ensure a regret uniformly bounded in time. More precisely, we construct an algorithm
  with a regret smaller than P i log(1/$\epsilon$) $\Delta$i , up to multiplicative
  constant and log log terms. We also prove a matching lower-bound, stating that no
  reasonable algorithm can outperform this quantity.
