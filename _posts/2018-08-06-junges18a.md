abstract: We study finite-state controllers (FSCs) for par- tially observable Markov
  decision processes (POMDPs) that are provably correct with re- spect to given specifications.
  The key in- sight is that computing (randomised) FSCs on POMDPs is equivalent to—and
  compu- tationally as hard as—synthesis for paramet- ric Markov chains (pMCs). This
  correspon- dence allows to use tools for synthesis in pMCs to compute correct-by-construction
  FSCs on POMDPs for a variety of specifications. Our experimental evaluation shows
  comparable per- formance to well-known POMDP solvers.
