abstract: We consider the weighted model counting task which includes important tasks
  in graphical models, such as computing the partition function and probability
  of evidence as special cases. We propose a novel partition-based bounding algorithm
  that exploits logical structure and gives rise to a set of inequalities from which
  upper (or lower) bounds can be derived efficiently. The bounds come with optimality
  guarantees under certain conditions and are oblivious in that they require only
  limited observations of the structure and parameters of the problem. We experimentally
  compare our bounds with the mini-bucket scheme (which is also oblivious) and show
  that our new bounds are often superior and never worse on a wide variety of benchmark
  networks.
