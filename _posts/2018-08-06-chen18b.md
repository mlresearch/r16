abstract: WTA (Winner Take All) hashing has been suc- cessfully applied in many large-scale
  vision applications. This hashing scheme was tai- lored to take advantage of the
  comparative rea- soning (or order based information), which showed significant accuracy
  improvements. In this paper, we identify a subtle issue with WTA, which grows with
  the sparsity of the datasets. This issue limits the discriminative power of WTA.
  We then propose a solution to this problem based on the idea of Densification which
  makes use of 2-universal hash functions in a novel way. Our experiments show that
  Densified WTA Hashing outperforms Vanilla WTA Hashing both in image retrieval and
  clas- sification tasks consistently and significantly.
