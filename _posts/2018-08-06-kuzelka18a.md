abstract: We consider the problem of predicting plausible missing facts in relational
  data, given a set of imperfect logical rules. In particular, our aim is to provide
  bounds on the (expected) number of incorrect inferences that are made in this way.
  Since for classical inference it is in general impossible to bound this number in
  a non-trivial way, we consider two inference relations that weaken, but remain close
  in spirit to classical inference.
