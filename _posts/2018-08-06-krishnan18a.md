abstract: We propose a new way to answer probabilis- tic queries that span multiple
  datapoints. We formalize reasoning about the similarity of dif- ferent datapoints
  as the evaluation of the Bayes Factor within a hierarchical deep generative model
  that enforces a separation between the latent variables used for representation
  learning and those used for reasoning. Under this model, we derive an intuitive
  estimator for the Bayes Factor that represents similarity as the amount of overlap
  in representation space shared by dif- ferent points. The estimator we derive relies
  on a query-conditional latent reasoning network, that parameterizes a distribution
  over the latent space of the deep generative model. The latent reasoning network
  is trained to amortize the posterior-predictive distribution under a hierar- chical
  model using supervised data and a max- margin learning algorithm. We explore how
  the model may be used to focus the data variations captured in the latent space
  of the deep genera- tive model and how this may be used to build new algorithms
  for few-shot learning.
