abstract: The essential graph is a distinguished member of a Markov equivalence class
  of AMP chain graphs. However, the directed edges in the es- sential graph are not
  necessarily strong or in- variant, i.e. they may not be shared by every member of
  the equivalence class. Likewise for the undirected edges. In this paper, we develop
  a procedure for identifying which edges in an essential graph are strong. We also
  show how this makes it possible to bound some causal ef- fects when the true chain
  graph is unknown.
